Soferio Reader · Mac, iPhone and iPad

Link to the exact spot in a PDF.

No more “see page 47, about halfway down”. With Soferio Reader, draw a box around a paragraph, table or figure and get a link to that exact spot. Paste it into your notes, an email, a task or another PDF. Click it later and the PDF opens right there, with the spot flashing so you can’t miss it.

  • Get Link, paste, click. Three simple steps from any page to the exact passage you need.
  • One link, every device. With Google Drive, links keep working even when files are moved or renamed.
  • Built for people who live in documents. Highlighting and drawing, search, on-device text recognition for scans, and links between PDFs.
  • Private by design. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your files stay on your device or in your own Google Drive.

Start free with documents up to 50 pages, or go Pro for no limits with one subscription across Mac, iPhone and iPad.

SenGram

Grammar, made into a game. SenGram challenges you to solve sentence diagram puzzles on iPhone and iPad, turning the structure of English into something you can see, move and understand.

  • Drag words into place to build sentence diagrams
  • Progress through levels, from simple subjects and verbs to challenge puzzles
  • Great for students, teachers, ESL learners and lovers of language
