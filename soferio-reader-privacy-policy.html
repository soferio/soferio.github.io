Soferio Reader: Privacy Policy

Soferio Pty Ltd · Last updated 22 November 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Soferio Reader does not send us your documents, your links, your annotations or any information about you or how you use the app. It has no accounts with us, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Your documents

Your PDFs stay on your device, where you keep them. Text recognition (OCR) is done on your device.

Google Drive

Using Google Drive is optional. If you use it, you sign in and choose files on Google's own pages. Soferio Reader can access only the files you open or choose with it. It downloads a chosen file from Google Drive to your device, and uploads it back to your Google Drive when you choose Save. These exchanges are directly between your device and Google; we never receive your files or your Google account details.

Downloaded copies are deleted when the app closes. Your sign-in is kept in your device's keychain until you choose Sign Out in the app's Settings.

Soferio Reader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
